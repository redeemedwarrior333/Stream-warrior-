Abuse often gets worse the moment he notices the surveillance is about to be cut off. Do these steps quietly, in order, and tell at least one trusted person what's happening. Make a plan with a hotline or a local advocate before you make your move.

📲 Step 1 · Get the free app

Phone Warrior is an Android app. There is a web guide on this page you can use right now on any phone — and the Android app adds the automatic scan plus the secret vault.

  1. On this site's GitHub repo, open the Actions tab.
  2. Click the latest "Build Phone Warrior APK" run.
  3. Download the PhoneWarrior-debug-apk artifact.
  4. On your phone, open the .apk and install it (allow "install unknown apps" if asked).
  5. First open: tap SCAN MY PHONE NOW.

The app has NO internet permission — it physically cannot send anything it finds to anyone. What it sees stays on your phone.

🔍 Step 2 · What the scan looks for

  • Known stalkerware families by name and signature
  • Apps using accessibility services to read your screen and messages
  • Device-admin / device-owner apps that won't let you uninstall things
  • Apps with notification access (he can see every text)
  • Hidden apps with no icon that have your location, mic or camera
  • Apps installed from outside the Play Store with spy-like names

Screenshot anything suspicious before uninstalling — that's evidence if you need a protective order later.

🧹 Step 3 · Clean up without tipping him off

Change passwords from a different device, never this phone. Email first — your email is the master key to everything else. Then banking, then socials. Turn on two-factor with an authenticator app, never SMS codes (they arrive on this phone — the one he may be reading). Check your email's forwarding rules and recovery phone/email: that's the classic trick for stealing your reset messages.

✉️ Step 4 · Make an email he can NEVER take over

If he takes every account you make, it's because he's breaking in through the recovery email and recovery phone you've had since before him. Start over the right way — from a clean device he has never touched (library computer, work computer, trusted friend's phone):

  1. Create the new address on that clean device. Leave your real name and birthday out of it.
  2. Recovery email: set it to a SECOND brand-new secret account — never an old one he's seen.
  3. Recovery phone: NEVER your old number. Get a free new number (Google Voice / TextNow) from that same clean device — or skip the phone entirely.
  4. Security answers: make them up. He knows your real ones.
  5. 2FA: authenticator app only — never SMS to your old number.
  6. Password: brand new, never used anywhere before.
  7. Then in the new account: sign out all devices, check no forwarding rules, review third-party app access.
  8. Keep the new addresses written only in the app's 🔒 Vault and on hidden paper. Never sign into the new accounts on a device he can reach.

🔒 Step 5 · The secret vault

The app has a hidden, encrypted vault for the things you can't afford to lose — new email addresses, hotline case numbers, advocate contacts, the address of a safe place. Everything is encrypted with your PIN (AES) and it all stays on your phone.

3 wrong PIN tries and it goes on LOCKDOWN — the PIN stops working on purpose, and only the Recovery Key you wrote down when you created it can open it again. No wipe button, no way for anyone forcing you to destroy what's inside, and no override — not for anyone who takes your phone, and not even for us.

🥸 Disguise mode

One tap inside the app swaps its home-screen icon and name to a boring gray "Notes" app. The icon hides; the protection doesn't.

📱 On an iPhone?

iPhones are much harder to bug without your Apple ID password — so changing that password from another device is your strongest move. Then: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management (delete any profile you don't recognize), audit your Apple ID device list, check Location Sharing and Family Sharing. Apps like Cydia, Sileo or Zebra mean the phone may be jailbroken — erase it and set up fresh from a backup that only has photos and messages.

Weird Law or Fake?

Fifty states of genuinely bizarre laws — mixed with fakes we made up to keep you honest. Real law or total nonsense?

How to play

You get a law from a random state. Hit REAL if you think it's actually on the books, FAKE if you smell nonsense. Right answers score a point, wrong ones drop you back one — streaks are where the bragging rights live. Your score is saved on your device.

📺 On stream?

This pairs perfectly with the Stream Warrior app's Weird Laws category — have chat vote REAL or FAKE in the comments before you lock in an answer. Chaos guaranteed.

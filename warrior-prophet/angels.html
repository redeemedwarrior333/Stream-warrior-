👋 What are angel numbers?

Many believers notice repeating numbers — 11:11 on the clock, 444 on a receipt — and take them as gentle nudges from heaven: pay attention, God is moving. Scripture shows God speaking through dreams, signs and wonders. An angel number is not a fortune; it is an invitation to pray. Always weigh every sign against God's Word (Acts 17:11).
